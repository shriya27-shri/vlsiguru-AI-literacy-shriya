## 3. How These Sources Were Used

- **Concept explanations:** The Google resources support foundational explanations of machine learning, data, model training, evaluation, and appropriate use cases.
- **Evaluation and failure analysis:** The Google resources support the discussion of training and test data, model performance, generalisation, and common evaluation concerns.
- **Semiconductor case study:** The two research publications provide examples of ML approaches to timing prediction and physical-design optimization.
- **Limitations:** Published experimental results apply to the methods and conditions studied. They should not be assumed to guarantee the same performance on every chip design or production workflow.
