# Mission 1 — Find AI Around Me

## 1. Everyday features

| Feature                        | What it does                                                                       | Initial classification                        |
| ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| YouTube recommendations        | Suggests videos based on my viewing activity and other signals.                    | Machine-learning-based AI                     |
| Google Maps traffic prediction | Uses traffic information to estimate journey times and predict traffic conditions. | Uses machine learning alongside other methods |
| Google Gemini                  | Generates answers and other content in response to my prompts.                     | Generative AI                                 |

## 2. Evidence

**Example 1: YouTube**

Source: YouTube Help — How YouTube recommendations work
Link: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16089387?hl=en

The official explanation describes how YouTube uses watch history, search history, subscriptions, likes and other signals to personalise recommendations. This supports classifying its recommendation system as machine-learning-based AI.

**Example 2: Google Maps**

Source: Google DeepMind — Traffic prediction with advanced Graph Neural Networks
Link: https://deepmind.google/blog/traffic-prediction-with-advanced-graph-neural-networks/

The source explains that machine learning combines historical traffic patterns with live traffic conditions to predict future traffic. This supports the use of AI/ML in traffic prediction.

**Example 3: Google Gemini**

Source: Google Gemini Apps Help — Learn about generative AI
Link: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13954172?hl=en-IN

The source explains how generative AI models create content and how language models generate responses based on patterns learned during training.

## 3. Could a rule-based program do something similar?

A simple rule-based program could recommend videos using fixed rules, estimate travel time using a fixed speed, or respond to predefined questions. However, reproducing personalised recommendations, changing traffic predictions, or flexible responses to many different prompts would require more than a few simple rules.

## 4. Conclusion

I use AI-enabled features in my daily life without always thinking about the technology behind them. Researching YouTube, Google Maps and Gemini helped me understand that AI can recommend content, predict changing conditions and generate new responses. I also learned that a feature can combine AI with traditional software rather than relying on AI alone.
