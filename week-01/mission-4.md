## Comparing Gemini and Claude

I asked Gemini and Claude to explain semiconductors at both a beginner level and a technical engineering level.

Both assistants explained conductivity, energy bands, doping, electron-hole pairs and the p–n junction. Their beginner explanations used familiar comparisons, while their technical explanations included equations and semiconductor physics.

I observed that the wording and level of detail can change depending on the prompt. Technical explanations can be useful for engineering study, but they also require careful verification.

During verification, I identified two points requiring caution. First, the built-in potential of a p–n junction is not universally 0.7 V. Second, a MOSFET's operation is primarily based on electric-field control of a channel, rather than simply the operation of a p–n junction diode.

**Conclusion:** 
AI assistants can help explain technical concepts at different levels, but their answers should be checked against reliable engineering references before being used in technical work.
