# Mission 9 — My AI-Use Rules

## My Five Rules for Using AI in Engineering Learning

### Rule 1: Verify important technical claims
I will check important technical explanations, equations, and engineering recommendations against reliable sources instead of trusting an AI answer automatically.

**Why:** In Mission 5, I checked the silicon bandgap claim and learned that a statement can be broadly correct but still need qualifications about precision and operating conditions.

### Rule 2: Protect confidential information
I will not enter confidential company information, proprietary chip designs, private test reports, passwords, or restricted project data into AI tools unless the organisation explicitly authorises that use.

### Rule 3: Understand the work I submit
I will make sure I understand AI-assisted answers, diagrams, and code before including them in my portfolio. I will be responsible for the accuracy and quality of my final work.

### Rule 4: Use AI to learn, not just to copy
I will ask AI to explain concepts, provide examples, challenge my understanding, and help me debug. I will then rewrite the key ideas in my own words and try to explain them independently.

### Rule 5: Test before applying AI-generated solutions
For practical engineering tasks, I will test generated code, check assumptions, and evaluate results before using them. I will seek expert guidance when the consequences of an error are significant.

## My Commitment

I will use AI as a learning and problem-solving assistant, not as a substitute for engineering judgment. I will question its answers, verify important claims, protect sensitive information, and take responsibility for the decisions I make.
