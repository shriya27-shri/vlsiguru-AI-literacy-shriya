# Mission 8 — Where Could AI Help My VLSI Track?

**VLSI track:** Design for Testability (DFT)

**Task:** Analysing scan-test or ATPG reports to identify repeated failure patterns.

**How AI could help:** AI could summarise large test reports, group similar failure patterns, and suggest possible causes for further investigation.

**Why human expertise still matters:** A DFT engineer must verify these suggestions using the design architecture, scan-chain information, fault model, test constraints, and actual test results before deciding on the cause or taking action.

**Conclusion:** AI can help speed up repetitive report analysis, but engineering knowledge and verification are essential for reliable DFT decisions.
